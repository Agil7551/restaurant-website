Our story

The Glen opened its doors in 2015 as a small breakfast counter with six stools and a single griddle. Word spread about the pancakes, and within a few years we had grown into a full bistro serving breakfast, lunch and dinner.

We're still family owned and operated, and many of the recipes on our menu come straight from our grandparents' kitchens.

Our kitchen

Everything is made from scratch each morning: breads, sauces, soups and desserts. We buy our produce, eggs and meat from farms within fifty miles of the restaurant, so the menu changes a little with every season.

Our head chef trained in classic French cooking and brings that care to every plate, whether it's a Sunday omelette or a Saturday night steak.

Visit us

Our dining room seats sixty, with a quiet patio for warmer days. We welcome walk-ins and take reservations for groups of six or more.

Open every day from 7am to 10pm. Have a question or want to book a private event? Send us a message.
